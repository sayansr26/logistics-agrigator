Logistics Aggregator Portal - Features Documentation
This document outlines the key features of the Logistics Aggregator Portal, a one-stop logistics management solution designed for e-Commerce, B2B, and B2C enterprises, as well as courier partners. It provides complete transparency for managing shipments, payments, disputes, tracking, and integrations.

1. Shipment Creation
   This module offers flexible options for creating new shipments.
   Bulk & Single Creation: Create individual shipments manually or upload a batch of shipments using Excel or CSV files.
   API / Plugin Integration: Automatically fetch orders from popular e-Commerce platforms like WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento, and OpenCart, or use custom APIs.
   Courier Selection: Compare real-time rates and choose the best courier partner based on criteria such as cost, delivery time (TAT - Turnaround Time), or service zone.
   Label & Manifest Generation: The system automatically generates shipping labels, invoices, and manifests for each shipment.
   Pickup Scheduling: Schedule real-time pickup requests directly with courier partners.
   Order Sync & Auto-Allocation: Automatically assign a courier partner based on pre-set rules, including zone, cost, distance, or service type.

2. Dispute Management
   Efficiently handle and resolve issues related to shipments.
   Dispute Raiser: Raise disputes for common issues like weight discrepancies, undelivered, damaged, or lost shipments.
   Ticket System: Use a centralized ticketing system to create and track disputes with courier partners.
   Evidence Upload: Attach supporting documents such as Proof of Delivery (POD), images, or other relevant files.
   Status Updates: Receive automated notifications on the progress and resolution of disputes.
   Resolution SLA Tracking: Monitor Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and use an escalation mechanism for pending disputes.

3. NDR (Non-Delivery Report) Management
   Gain control over undelivered shipments with a centralized system.
   NDR Dashboard: View all non-delivery reasons and their statuses in a single, centralized dashboard.
   Action Panel: Take immediate action by marking a shipment for reattempt, address correction, future delivery, or return-to-origin (RTO).
   Auto-Communication: The system automatically sends updates to customers via SMS, WhatsApp, or email to confirm delivery details.
   Performance Analytics: Analyze courier-wise NDR percentages and resolution rates to identify trends.
   Customer Feedback Capture: Collect customer input on failed deliveries to improve future service.

4. Tracking & Notifications
   Provide full transparency on shipment status from pickup to delivery.
   Real-Time Tracking: Track shipments using a AWB (Air Waybill) number, order ID, or client reference number.
   Unified Dashboard: Track all courier partners from one single panel.
   Customer Updates: Send automated SMS, email, or WhatsApp notifications to customers at each major shipment milestone.
   Branded Tracking Page: Offer a custom tracking link with your company's branding for a professional look.

5. Wallet System
   Manage all financial transactions securely and efficiently.
   Prepaid Wallet: Load your wallet balance and pay for shipments in real-time.
   Auto Recharge: Set up low-balance alerts or enable an auto top-up feature to ensure continuous service.
   Transaction History: View detailed credit and debit logs for all shipments and services.
   Multi-User Wallet Access: Manage permissions and access to the wallet for different client roles.
   Refund Adjustments: Automatically adjust charges for failed shipments or disputes directly into the wallet.

6. Remittance & Settlements
   Track and reconcile all payments, especially for COD shipments.
   COD Remittance Tracking: Access daily or weekly settlement reports for Cash on Delivery (COD) payments.
   Courier-wise Reconciliation: Easily check pending COD payments and settled amounts for each courier partner.
   Custom Settlement Rules: Define your own settlement cycles, such as T+2 or T+3 (transaction date plus two or three days).
   Mismatch Alerts: Receive automated alerts for any discrepancies in COD amounts.
   Client-Wise Settlements: Track payouts and settlements for each individual client account.

7. Invoice & Billing
   Simplify and automate your billing and invoicing processes.
   Automated Invoice Generation: The system generates client-wise invoices based on shipment statuses (delivered, in transit, RTO, manifested).
   Tax Compliant (GST Ready): Generate GSTR reports for easy tax reconciliation.
   Rate Card Management: Configure pricing rules based on zone, weight, distance, and courier.
   Downloadable Reports: Download invoices and reports in PDF, Excel, and CSV formats.
   Billing Dashboard: A comprehensive view of pending invoices, settlements, and adjustments.

8. Zone Creation & Management
   Create and manage custom delivery zones to optimize logistics.
   Custom Zone Creation: Define delivery zones by PIN codes, area, city, state, or distance slabs.
   Courier Mapping: Assign specific courier partners to pre-defined zones.
   Pricing Rules: Configure pricing based on weight slabs, distance, and zones.
   Partner-Wise Allocation: Define service areas for each partner based on location or distance.
   Dynamic Updates: The system automatically adjusts zones based on changes from courier partners or newly serviceable regions.

9. Partner Management
   Onboard, manage, and evaluate your courier partners.
   Courier Partner Onboarding: Easily add and manage multiple logistics partners within the portal.
   Rate & Zone Mapping: Configure specific rates and zones for each courier.
   Performance Metrics: Compare partners based on key metrics like SLA, TAT, delivery percentage, and RTO percentage.
   API Integration: Ensure real-time data synchronization with partner systems.
   Multi-Mode Support: Integrate with various courier types, including Surface, Air, Express, Hyperlocal, and Tnternational partners.

10. Client Management
    Manage multiple client accounts with ease.
    Multi-Client Accounts: Onboard and manage multiple sellers or brands from a single platform.
    Access Control: Assign specific roles and permissions (Admin, Finance, Operations) to control user access.
    Billing & Settlements: Access client-wise invoices, COD, MIS (Management Information System), and wallet history.
    Performance Dashboard: View analytics for each client, including shipment count, RTO percentage, and revenue.
    White-Label Branding: Offer a custom-branded portal to clients with their own logo and brand name.

11. Warehouse & Fulfillment
    Manage and track all your pickup locations.
    Address Management: Add and manage an unlimited number of pickup addresses, including warehouses, stores, offices, or vendor locations.

12. Support Desk
    Provide comprehensive support to all users.
    Ticketing System: Users can raise and track issues related to technical problems, courier services, or billing.
    Knowledge Base: Access a library of FAQs, SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures), and process documents.
    Live Chat & Email Support: Integrated communication panel for real-time and asynchronous support.
    SLA Tracking: Monitor response and resolution times to ensure timely support.

13. Reporting & MIS
    Generate insightful reports for better decision-making.
    Shipment Reports: Generate reports on delivered, in-transit, RTO, pending, and lost shipments.
    Financial Reports: Access detailed reports on COD, wallet transactions, invoices, and dispute logs.
    Partner Performance: Analyze courier efficiency and SLA adherence to optimize your logistics network.
    Client-Wise Reports: Gain business insights for each client or brand.

14. Security & User Roles
    Protect sensitive data with robust security features.
    Role-Based Access: Assign predefined roles (Admin, Finance, Operations, Client, Support) to control user access and permissions.
    Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Enhance account security with an extra layer of verification.
    Audit Logs: Track and log every activity within the portal for complete transparency and accountability.
    Data Encryption: Ensure all API communication and wallet transactions are secure and encrypted.
