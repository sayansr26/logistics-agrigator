Journey 01 · Book a shipment

From "I need to ship this" to a printed label

The booking wizard walks through the parcel step by step, then shows every courier's price side by side so the cheapest or fastest is one click away.

  1. 1

    Enter the delivery

    Who's it going to and where. Type a pincode and the city, state and serviceability fill themselves in.

  2. 2

    Describe the parcel

    Weight, dimensions and value. The panel works out the chargeable weight automatically — the higher of actual and volumetric.

  3. 3

    Add the invoice

    Contents, HSN and GST for a compliant shipping label and paperwork.

  4. 4

    Compare & pick a courier

    Live rates from every serviceable carrier, ranked. Choose on price, speed or COD support.

  5. 5

    Confirm

    The fee is held from the wallet, the courier is booked, and an AWB tracking number is generated instantly.

Journey 02 · When the courier re-weighs

The parcel weighed more. The panel sorts out the money.

Couriers re-measure parcels at their hub. When the weight changes, the panel re-prices the shipment, works out the new profit, and squares the wallet automatically — no spreadsheets.

  1. 1

    New weight comes in

    Enter the courier's actual weight and dimensions — or upload a whole spreadsheet of them at once.

  2. 2

    Charges recalculate

    Selling price, courier cost and profit margin all update together. Preview the before-and-after before committing.

  3. 3

    Wallet auto-reconciles

    The old fee is refunded and the new one charged — so the shipper is only ever out the correct amount.

  4. 4

    Do it in bulk

    Upload a CSV of AWBs and new weights; every parcel is re-rated and every wallet squared, with a pass/fail report.

Journey 03 · Carrier channels

One courier, many accounts — the panel routes each parcel to the right one

A single carrier like Delhivery often has several accounts, each priced for a different weight band. Set the slabs once, and every booking is sent through the account that fits its chargeable weight.

Define the slabs and accounts per courier. Admins manage them from the partner screen.

The settlement approval trail

Every payout passes through these gates

Pay how you like

Release to the shipper's wallet instantly, or record a bank / UPI transfer with its reference.

Adjust for reality

Lost or damaged parcels, short collections, credit & debit notes — all fold into the final payable.
