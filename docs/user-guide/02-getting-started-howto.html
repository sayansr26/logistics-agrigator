Step 0 · Before you begin

What you'll need in hand

Gather these first so the setup runs start to finish without stopping to hunt for details.

Your login

The account & licence for your company, and admin access to invite your team.

Courier credentials

API keys / account details for each courier you'll ship with (Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC…).

Money to load

A way to top up the wallet — bookings hold the fee from it, so it can't be empty.

Pricing & GST

Your selling rates, courier costs, and GST/GSTIN details for compliant invoices.

Payout details

Bank / UPI info if you'll settle COD outside the wallet, and your settlement cycle preference.

First-time setup · steps 1 – 6

The six things to configure, in order

Tap any step to expand it. Tick items off as you go — your progress is saved on this device. Do them top to bottom: each one depends on the ones above it.

Why the order matters: you can't book a shipment until a courier exists, it can't be priced without rate rules, and the fee can't be charged without a funded wallet. Settlement rules come last because they only act on delivered COD parcels.

1

Create your team

AdminUsers & Outlets · 10 min
You'll need: names & emails of your staff

  • Open User Management and invite each person — finance, sales, support.

  • Give each one a role. The role decides what they can see and do (finance gets settlements; sales gets bookings).

  • Add your outlets / warehouses in Outlet Management — these are the shippers who create parcels.

2

Add your courier partners

AdminCourier Partners · 15 min
You'll need: courier API keys / account credentials

  • Go to Courier Partners and add each courier you'll use.

  • Enter its API credentials so the panel can book, print labels and track in real time.

  • Mark whether it supports COD and set its serviceable areas.

3

Set carrier accounts (weight slabs)

AdminPartner → Carrier Accounts · 10 min
You'll need: your account-per-weight-band arrangement with the courier

  • For couriers with more than one account, open its Carrier Accounts and add a channel per band.

  • Set each channel's weight slab (e.g. 0–5 kg, 5–20 kg) and which account it uses.

  • Done once, the panel routes every parcel automatically to the account that fits its weight.

4

Build your pricing

AdminConfiguration · 25 min
You'll need: your selling rates and courier cost rates

  • Define zones & pincode types in Geography so distance-based pricing works.

  • Add charge rules in Charges — per-kg, weight-slab or zone-to-zone. Include the courier cost side so profit is tracked.

  • Optionally create discount packages (badge tiers) for your best customers.

5

Fund the wallet

Client / FinanceWallet · 5 min
You'll need: a payment method to top up

  • Open Wallet and load a balance. Every booking holds its fee here.

  • Keep enough buffer — a low wallet puts new shipments on hold until topped up.

6

Set COD settlement rules

FinanceCOD Settlements → Rules · 10 min
You'll need: your payout cycle & method

  • Choose a settlement cycle — daily, weekly, T+1 or T+2 — per customer or across the board.

  • Pick the payout method: wallet credit, bank transfer or UPI.

  • Turn on auto-release if you want the panel to pay out on schedule without a manual click.

Setup done. With a team, couriers, pricing and a funded wallet in place, you're ready to ship. Everything below is day-to-day.

Everyday tasks · recipes

How to do the things you'll do every day

Each recipe is the exact sequence of clicks, where you'll be, and what you get at the end.

Book a shipment

Sales · Outlet
  1. New Shipment → enter the delivery address. Type the pincode; city & state auto-fill.
  2. Add weight & dimensions. Chargeable weight is worked out for you.
  3. Fill the invoice — contents, value, HSN, GST.
  4. Compare couriers and pick one on price or speed.
  5. Confirm. The fee is held; a courier is booked.
Result: an AWB tracking number and a printable label.

Create shipments in bulk

Sales · Outlet
  1. Go to Shipments → Bulk and download the template.
  2. Fill one row per parcel; upload the file.
  3. The panel validates & books them, tracking progress live.
Result: a report of every parcel booked, with any failures flagged.

Track & fix a failed delivery

Support
  1. Track any parcel by AWB for its live status.
  2. Open NDR to see parcels that couldn't be delivered.
  3. Take an action — reattempt, update the address, or return.
Result: the courier retries or returns, and the customer is kept in the loop.

Re-weigh & re-price

Admin · Finance
  1. Open the shipment, enter the courier's real weight, and preview the new charges.
  2. Confirm — the wallet refunds the old fee and charges the new one.
  3. Many at once? Bulk Weight Update → upload a CSV of AWBs & weights.
Result: correct price, updated profit, and a squared-up wallet.

Bring in & reconcile COD

Finance
  1. In COD Shipments, Import Report — paste or upload the courier's CSV.
  2. Click Auto Reconcile to match every collection to its parcel.
  3. Resolve anything flagged short, excess, missing or duplicate.
Result: every rupee matched, mismatches surfaced for a human to clear.

Settle & pay the shipper

Finance
  1. In COD Settlements, Generate a settlement from reconciled parcels.
  2. Add any adjustments (lost, damaged, credit / debit notes).
  3. Walk it through Verify → Approve → Release.
Result: the shipper is paid to wallet or bank, with a full audit trail.

Tips & pitfalls

Small things that save big headaches

Keep the wallet topped up

A low balance quietly puts new bookings on hold. Set a reminder to refill before it runs dry.

Reconcile little and often

Import courier COD reports as they arrive, not monthly — mismatches are far easier to trace while fresh.

Preview before you re-rate

Always check the before/after on a weight change so the new price and profit are what you expect.

Let roles do the fencing

Don't share one login. Give each person their own role so access — and the audit trail — stays clean.

Automate the routine payouts

Turn on a settlement cycle so predictable payouts happen on their own; reserve manual release for exceptions.

Clear held settlements

Anything on hold is money not yet with the shipper. Make the Hold list a daily glance for finance.
